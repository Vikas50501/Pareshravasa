Shah Paresh & Associates is an established professional practice based in Mumbai, now exclusively focused on tax litigation and appellate work in Income-tax and GST matters. We represent taxpayers in disputes before appellate forums and courts across India.

The chamber operates on a model of personal principal involvement — every matter is handled directly by CA Paresh Shah, ensuring consistency, strategic depth and undivided professional attention at every stage of proceedings.

We are a dedicated appellate and litigation-focused practice, representing taxpayers in tax disputes and contentious proceedings. Independent of routine compliance work, the chamber focuses on strategic advocacy in appeals and tax controversy proceedings, with emphasis on procedural precision, careful preparation and principled representation.

CA Paresh Shah
Founder & Principal · FCA

CA Paresh Shah is a Fellow Chartered Accountant (ICAI) with over 35 years of continuous practice in Income-tax and State tax (now GST) litigation.

He is the Founder and Principal of Shah Paresh & Associates and is personally involved in every matter undertaken by the practice — from strategy formulation to representation at hearings. His career spans extensive representation before income-tax authorities, CIT(A), ITAT Mumbai and the Bombay High Court. Beyond litigation, he has been an active contributor to the CA profession through speaking engagements at WIRC of ICAI, technical publications and leadership of professional associations.
